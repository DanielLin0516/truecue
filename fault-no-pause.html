Problems and fixes

No pause at the back

You go straight from the backswing into the forward stroke, so any wobble in the backswing carries into the shot.

Common causes

  • Rushing the shot
  • Nerves under pressure

How to fix it

  1. Say "stop" in your head at the end of the backswing
  2. Practice with the pause drill beats
  3. Take one slow breath before you pull back
