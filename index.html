The three scores

Three scores, in words you already use

Every stroke gets a score from 0 to 100, plus cue speed, power and tempo. Everyone is scored on the same scale; choose how strict it is in Practice settings. Pull the cue back right after contact and the coach tells you. Short stop shots are never marked down.

Straight

Did your back hand move in or out, or your wrist twist, on the way to the ball?

Accelerate

Was the cue still speeding up at contact, or already slowing down?

Pause & Finish

Did you pause at the back, and freeze at the finish?

What it does

A coach on your wrist

How to fix it

Every fault comes with a plain explanation, the usual causes, a few fixes and a drill. A 3D demo shows the wrong way and the right way.

7 drills

Straight 10, Pause Drill, Stop Shot, Rhythm, Same Force, Power Ladder and Perfect 10. Each has a clear target, and you earn stickers as you go.

Match mode

Records every stroke with no taps and no scores on screen, so nothing distracts you in a real game.

Live wrist view

Move your hand and the cue on your iPhone moves with it. Handy while you calibrate.

Your iPhone

Session summaries, each stroke’s wrist path and speed curve, weekly trends and a share card when you finish.

On your watch face

A widget shows your last practice average.

Honest scores

No guessing

When a stroke is uncertain, you see “Not measured” instead of a score. No score is better than a wrong one.

Limits, said plainly

  • It measures your wrist, not the cue. A still wrist with a crooked cue will not show.
  • A loose strap makes the data noisy. Wear the watch snug.
  • Jump and massé shots, with the cue raised steeply, are not scored.
  • Millimetre values on the wrist path are estimates, for the picture only. Scores use the angle.
